Deployment Readiness Gate

Complete these shared prerequisites before enforcing labeling or DLP scenarios.

Validate licensing by feature and workload, not with one generic E3/E5 assumption.

Confirm Information Protection, DLP, Content Explorer, Audit, and delegated administration roles.

Business owners approve label names, priority, descriptions, encryption, and downgrade behaviour.

Confirm target users receive the expected label policy and client applications support it.

Enable sensitivity-label processing and validate supported file and encryption states.

Confirm Activity Explorer, Audit, alerts, Content Explorer roles, and retention requirements.

Administrators validate configuration; data owners validate whether sampled matches are truly sensitive.

Not started		 Historical SharePoint financial records remediation
SS-01		 Large volumes of unlabeled legacy financial files remain searchable and shareable without consistent protection. High-confidence financial SITs in eligible Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or PDF files stored in scoped SharePoint sites. Unclassified financial records create oversharing, audit, and incident-response gaps. Run a SharePoint service-side policy in simulation, validate matches with finance owners, then apply a protected financial label in controlled site waves. Historical content receives a persistent classification and can drive DLP, sharing, and audit controls. Service - SharePoint
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 OneDrive customer PII remediation
SS-02		 Personal working files in OneDrive contain customer PII that users did not label during creation. Customer identifiers, address data, or government IDs match high-confidence SIT conditions. Sensitive customer records may be shared externally or synchronized to unmanaged locations without a durable classification. Use a service-side OneDrive policy to label matching stored files and connect the label to DLP and sharing controls. Customer PII is classified consistently across user-owned storage without waiting for files to be reopened. Service - OneDrive
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 Exchange outbound payment-card protection
SS-03		 Employees can send cardholder data in email bodies or supported attachments without selecting the correct label. Credit Card Number SIT meets the configured confidence and instance threshold in outbound Exchange mail. Cardholder data exposure creates PCI response, notification, and contractual risk. Apply a PCI sensitivity label to matching email in transit and use encryption or DLP according to the approved handling model. Payment-card email is consistently classified at the service boundary; policy events are available for review. Service - Exchange in transit
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 Inbound healthcare email classification
SS-04		 External laboratories and partners send PHI that arrives without the organization's sensitivity labels. Named-entity or health-data SIT conditions match incoming Exchange Online email. Unlabeled inbound PHI weakens downstream DLP, investigation, and handling consistency. Include all Exchange locations as required for external senders, label matched inbound email, and configure encryption ownership only when the design requires it. Inbound PHI becomes visible to label-based controls immediately after Exchange processing. Service - Exchange in transit
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 PDF legal-contract classification in OneDrive

Contract trainable classifier or corroborated legal keywords match supported PDF files stored in OneDrive. Executed agreements may remain unclassified, increasing discovery and sharing risk. Use service-side PDF inspection with a legal-contract policy and apply an approved legal confidentiality label. Stored PDF contracts gain consistent classification without conversion to Office formats. Service - OneDrive

Not started		 OCR classification for scanned identity records
SS-06		 Scanned passports and identity documents are image-based and invisible to standard text-only inspection. OCR extracts supported text from eligible stored images or image-based documents and an identity SIT matches. Identity records can remain undiscovered and outside labeling or DLP controls. Enable the required OCR capability and billing model, test representative scans, then apply an identity-data label through service-side policy. Previously opaque scanned records become classifiable and governable. Service - SharePoint/OneDrive
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 Secure-by-default baseline for unlabeled repositories
SS-07		 The organization cannot wait for perfect classifiers before reducing broad exposure across unlabeled content. Eligible content is unlabeled or resides in a designated high-risk repository. A large unclassified estate remains available for oversharing and inconsistent downstream control. Apply an intuitive baseline label by default, then use higher-priority content conditions for stronger labels. The estate gains a minimum protection baseline while classification maturity continues. Service - SharePoint/OneDrive
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 CAD and engineering-file contextual labeling
SS-08		 Critical engineering drawings use binary or specialized formats that content inspection cannot parse reliably. Contextual conditions such as file extension, document name, location, or creator match the engineering pattern. High-value design IP can remain unclassified solely because its file type is not text-readable. Use service-side contextual conditions to label designated engineering file types or repositories, with exclusions for known benign content. Non-text engineering assets receive consistent classification based on defensible context. Service - SharePoint/OneDrive
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗

Not started		 Large-estate policy partitioning and wave rollout
SS-09		 A single policy across a very large SharePoint estate produces too many matches and too much operational risk for one activation. Simulation or location volume exceeds a manageable review and rollout boundary. Broad activation can cause avoidable labeling failures, access disruption, and slow remediation. Partition locations into non-overlapping policy groups, standardize rule names, require wave-level sign-off, and compare failures before expansion. Scale-out becomes supportable and each rollout wave has accountable owners and rollback criteria. Service - Phased enterprise rollout
Sensitivity labels ↗Auto-labeling policies ↗
